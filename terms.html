3. Eligibility Requirements

3.1 Candidate Eligibility

To apply as a Candidate, you must:

  • Be a female South African citizen or permanent resident (valid ID/passport required);
  • Be between 18 and 30 years old on the date of the Event (proof of age required);
  • Have never been married, had a marriage annulled, or cohabited in a marriage-like relationship;
  • Have no dependent children;
  • Be of good moral character with no criminal convictions (a police clearance certificate may be requested);
  • Meet the health and fitness standards required for pageant participation;
  • Not be currently contracted to any competing pageant or modelling agency that would conflict with Miss Club Model SA obligations;
  • Agree to comply with all rules, schedules, and code of conduct issued by the Organisation.

3.2 Venue Partner Eligibility

To qualify as a Venue Partner, the venue must:

  • Be a legally registered business entity in South Africa (CIPC registration required);
  • Hold valid public liability insurance (minimum R10 million);
  • Comply with all applicable South African health, safety, and fire regulations;
  • Have a minimum capacity of 200 guests with appropriate facilities;
  • Provide accessibility for persons with disabilities per the Promotion of Equality and Prevention of Unfair Discrimination Act 4 of 2000;
  • Not be subject to any legal proceedings that would impair its ability to host events.

4. Candidate Application Terms

4.1 Application Process

Applications must be submitted via the official Website and include:

  • Completed application form with accurate personal details;
  • Certified copy of South African ID or passport;
  • Recent headshot and full-length photographs (specifications provided on the application portal);
  • 60-second video introduction;
  • Personal platform/advocacy statement (maximum 500 words);
  • Personal essay: "Why should you win Miss Club Model SA?" (maximum 1000 words);
  • Talent description (if applicable);
  • Emergency contact details;
  • Signed declaration of eligibility and media release (included in the application form).

4.2 Application Fees

An application fee may be required as published on the Website for each Event. Fees are non-refundable except where an Event is cancelled by the Organisation, in which case a full refund will be issued within 30 business days.

4.3 Selection Process

  • Applications are reviewed by the selection panel based on: poise, communication skills, advocacy platform, community involvement, and overall presentation.
  • The Organisation reserves the right to request additional information, conduct interviews, or require auditions.
  • Decisions of the selection panel are final and binding. No correspondence will be entered into regarding unsuccessful applications.
  • Selected Candidates must sign a formal Candidate Agreement before participating in any Event.

4.4 Candidate Obligations

If selected, Candidates agree to:

  • Attend all mandatory rehearsals, workshops, and Events as scheduled;
  • Maintain the eligibility criteria throughout the competition period;
  • Adhere to the Miss Club Model SA Code of Conduct (provided upon selection);
  • Obtain written permission from the Organisation before accepting other pageant titles, modelling contracts, or media appearances during the reign;
  • Represent the Organisation with dignity, professionalism, and in alignment with its values;
  • Participate in promotional activities, media interviews, and community engagements as reasonably required.
