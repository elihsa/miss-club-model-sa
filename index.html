About the Pageant

Miss Club Model SA is more than a beauty pageant — it is a celebration of character, leadership, and purpose. Founded to uplift and empower women, our pageant seeks candidates who embody grace, intelligence, and a commitment to positive change.

Our judging criteria evaluate poise, communication, community involvement, and personal platform. We believe true beauty shines through action and advocacy.

Our Vision: To crown ambassadors who will represent South Africa with dignity and drive meaningful social impact.
