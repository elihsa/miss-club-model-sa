1. Introduction & Scope

Miss Club Model SA ("we," "us," "our," "the Organisation") is committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), South Africa's comprehensive data protection law.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your Personal Information when you:

  • Visit our website at missclubmodel.vercel.app;
  • Apply as a Candidate for Miss Club Model SA pageants;
  • Register for Events or join waitlists;
  • Submit a Venue Partner application;
  • Subscribe to our newsletter;
  • Interact with us via email, phone, or social media.
POPIA Compliance: This policy is designed to meet the requirements of POPIA Sections 11–25 (conditions for lawful processing), Section 18 (notification to data subjects), Section 19 (security safeguards), Sections 23–25 (data subject rights), and Sections 57–59 (prior authorisation for special personal information and cross-border transfers).

5. Children's Information (POPIA Section 34)

Miss Club Model SA Events are strictly for individuals aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from children under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected a child's information, contact us immediately at privacy@missclubmodelsa.co.za and we will delete it.

9. Cross-Border Transfers (POPIA Sections 57–59)

Our service providers may process data outside South Africa:

  • Vercel (Hosting/CDN): United States / Global edge network — Standard Contractual Clauses + adequacy assessment;
  • Supabase (Database/Auth): United States / EU — Standard Contractual Clauses + SOC 2 Type II;
  • Resend (Email): United States — Standard Contractual Clauses;
  • Google Fonts/Analytics: United States — IP anonymisation, consent-based loading.
POPIA Section 59: Cross-border transfers are permitted where the recipient is subject to a law, binding corporate rules, or binding agreement providing an adequate level of protection. We have executed Data Processing Agreements with Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the Information Regulator for all cross-border processors.

14. Automated Decision-Making & Profiling (POPIA Section 71)

We do not use fully automated decision-making that produces legal effects or significantly affects you (e.g., automated Candidate selection). All selection decisions involve human judgement by the judging panel.

We may use profiling for:

  • Diversity analytics (aggregated, anonymised);
  • Marketing segmentation (with consent);
  • Security anomaly detection (IP patterns, failed logins).

You have the right to request human review of any automated processing that affects you.

15. Data Breach Notification (POPIA Section 22)

In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in risk to your rights and freedoms:

  1. We will notify the Information Regulator within 72 hours of becoming aware;
  2. We will notify affected data subjects without undue delay (typically within 72 hours) where there is a high risk;
  3. Notifications will include: nature of breach, categories of data affected, approximate number of subjects, likely consequences, measures taken, and contact details for the Information Officer.

We maintain a Data Breach Register and conduct post-incident reviews.
